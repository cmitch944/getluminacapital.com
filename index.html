How It Works

Five steps between a dead file and a live lender conversation. You stay in control at every one of them — the platform does the searching, you make the calls.

Step 1

Subscribe

Prepaid monthly through Stripe. Founding Brokers lock $149/mo for life — the first 10 only. No lock-in contract; cancel any time.

Step 2

Upload the deal

Drop in the deal file. AI Underwriting reads it and extracts the parameters that decide placement — amount, industry, credit grade, time in business, and the rest.

Step 3

Confirm the parameters

Nothing runs on unconfirmed data. You review what the AI extracted, fix anything it got wrong, and approve the deal profile before matching.

Step 4

Get exactly 3 recommendations

Each comes with the why: lender type and profile fit, deal-size band, credit grades served, indicative rate and term ranges, HQ region. Names stay anonymous at this stage.

Step 5

Choose one, connect

Pick the lender to pursue for that deal. Only then is that one lender's identity and public contact route released — in-platform, for that deal, and logged. Track the deal from Submitted to Funded inside the platform.

Meet Luna Desk™ — the agent that works your deals

Matching is only half the job. Luna Desk™ is Lumina Capital's built-in loan officer agent: it does the chasing, collecting, and packaging, while you keep every key decision — the lender choice, the package, the terms, and every client-facing message.

Reactivation

Your dead list, back to life

Import your previous-client list and Luna Desk™ runs a reactivation campaign to them — email first, texting only where consent is on file, opt-outs honored immediately. Responses open an intake automatically.

Intake & docs

The file builds itself

Luna Desk™ gathers the deal basics from your client and collects the bank statements and documents into the deal file — which feeds the same AI Underwriting and matching, with you confirming the parameters before anything runs.

Appointments

No dead-end submissions

Most funders require a signed ISO agreement before they take a deal. Every recommendation shows your appointment status with that lender, and if you're not signed up with the one you pick, the signup step comes first — your package never goes out cold, and your commission stays protected.

Submission

You approve, it sends

Luna Desk™ assembles the standardized submission package for your chosen lender. Nothing is recorded as sent until you approve the package — and every send is logged.

Approval & funding

Terms land with you first

When a funder approves, you get the alert with the terms. Nothing reaches your client until you approve them. Then Luna Desk™ delivers the terms, collects what's needed, and the contract is tracked through Funded.

Protected by design

A lender database anyone can copy is worth nothing within a month. Lumina Capital is built so the asset — and your edge as a subscriber — survives.

The database is never browsable, searchable, or exportable. Recommendations are profiles first; a lender's identity is released only for the one lender you select for a specific deal, and every release is logged. Submission quotas and probing detection keep the matching honest. You build your own relationship with the lender you choose — that's the point — but the map itself stays ours, so it keeps working for you deal after deal.
